About The Founder


Shariz was founded by Dr Innocent Eddo, a visionary leader with a deep passion for technology and education. With over 8 years of experience in the tech industry,Dr Innocent Eddo recognized the transformative power of technology and the importance of making tech education accessible to all.
Motivated by a desire to give back to the community and bridge the digital divide, Dr Innocent established Shariz with a clear vision:
To empower individuals with the knowledge and skills needed to thrive in the digital age. His goal was to create an inclusive environment where people from all backgrounds could access quality tech education and unlock their potential. Beyond his professional achievements, Dr Innocent is dedicated to continuous learning and personal growth. He is an advocate for lifelong learning, and a mentor to aspiring tech professionals.

With a relentless commitment to transforming lives through tech education, He continues to lead Shariz towards a future where everyone has the opportunity to succeed in the digital world.
